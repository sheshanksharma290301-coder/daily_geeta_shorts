# Style Guide

## Voice
- Male, shaant, slow pace, gambhir tone.
- Sanskrit aur Hindi ke beech 0.5-1 sec ka pause.
- Roz same voice (channel ki pehchaan).
- Pehle 5-10 shlokas par pronunciation test. Galat ho to alag voice/settings try karna.

## Visual (Higgsfield)
- Fixed **style prompt** (saare videos ek jaise lagein):
  > "Cinematic, serene, divine atmosphere, golden light, soft particles, traditional Indian art inspired, Krishna and Arjun on chariot at Kurukshetra, vertical 9:16"
- Shloka ke theme ke hisaab se sirf scene badalna, style same.
- Clips 5-10 sec, slow camera movement.
- Cost control: clips reuse/loop karna, weekly batch generation consider karna.

## Subtitles
- Devanagari, **video edit stage par render hote hain** (AI video me nahi, kyunki AI Devanagari text bigaad deta hai).
- Font: Noto Sans Devanagari (ya similar).
- Bottom me Reels/Shorts UI ke safe area se bahar.

## Audio
- Voice clear, background music (flute/tanpura) halka.
- Loudness normalize (ffmpeg loudnorm).
- Music royalty-free hona chahiye (source pending).
